Two Maps, One Lesson — infrastructure creates price

Prices are not created by commodities. Prices are created by infrastructure constraints. The molecule is identical at Waha and Algonquin, at PVB and TTF, at Wallumbilla and Longford; the electron is identical at NP15 and SP15, in NO2 and NO4, in Victoria and Tasmania. The infrastructure between them is not — and the basis is the price of that difference.
